# Build log

What I built with Kiro, why, and what was measured, in date order. Neuron figures are the benchmark's or the Worker's estimates from measured tokens (with a 1.1 safety margin on the Worker's) unless marked as a dashboard reading. Run-by-run accounting, review rounds and freeze notes are kept unchanged in [`docs/archive/`](archive/); the results that back claims in the README are in [`docs/results/`](results/).

## 1 Nov 2026: engine, Worker, benchmark

**Monorepo (T1).** npm workspaces with shared TypeScript, ESLint and Vitest config; engine, UI, two apps, Worker and benchmark packages; CI. A boundary test and a lint rule keep domain words out of the engine (R1.3).

**Engine core (T2).** Evidence validation with original-text offsets, value-support checks (amount, date, quantity, name, text), corrections with provenance, processing-versus-coverage tracking, matcher, finding fingerprints, review reopening, export model. 50 tests. Mutation check: disabling the coverage gate fails the "extraction misses a line" test.

**Extraction Worker (T3).** Server-controlled profiles, strict request validation, origin allowlist, a SQLite Durable Object admission counter (every attempt admitted first, never refunded, fails closed), distinct error codes, content-free logs. 94 tests, including 60 parallel requests against ceiling 10 (exactly 10 admitted), restart persistence and the 00:00 UTC rollover. I shipped it closed (`DAILY_ADMISSION_CEILING=0`) until the benchmark measured cost per call. Dependency audit: root overrides pin patched `undici@7.30.1` and `sharp@0.35.5` (0 high remain); 11 moderate remain in the ESLint chain (`minimatch`), dev tooling only, not deployed.

**Samples and benchmark (T4).** Fictional labelled samples (ScopeCheck: RFQ and two quotations; PackCheck: four documents), PDF text blocks with page and coordinates (scans rejected), and a harness with scoring. Mock oracle first (recall 100%, 0 false critical). Then 11 live attempts with `@cf/meta/llama-3.3-70b-instruct-fp8-fast`: every request schema-valid, evidence located 100%, no unsupported value accepted. Faults kept in view: item codes dropped on the RFQ and quote-b; "R1.6 million" returned as 1.6 under `packcheck.grant.v1` (held by validation) and as 1,600,000 ZAR under `v2`. Strict findings: ScopeCheck 4 of 4 after the shared item-identity resolver (1 of 4 before), PackCheck 3 of 3, layout variant 4 of 5. Dashboard checkpoint: 462.81 neurons after 5 attempts against an estimate of 462.76.

**Model decision (5.3, 5.4).** Criteria committed before the run. `@cf/meta/llama-3.1-8b-instruct` on the same eight documents: 21 missed labelled facts against 1 for the 70B, strict findings 3 of 4 (70B 4 of 4), layout 2 of 5 (70B 4 of 5), PackCheck equal. I kept the 70B (`docs/model-comparison.md`). Production admission stayed 0: eight tiny documents do not justify a public ceiling.

**App shells on fixtures (T6).** Both apps run in sample mode from fixtures (source blocks plus the model's recorded claims); rules, review, corrections and export run in the browser, and a test checks that no saved findings are stored. Added the comparison ledger (agree / flagged / not evaluated with reason and blocked input), text and CSV exports (CSV neutralises spreadsheet formulas), and adapters shared with the benchmark so what is measured is what ships. 209+ tests; mutation checks: reporting a held value as "agree" (7 failures), bypassing the identity resolver (15), never reopening decisions (3).

**Review rounds.** An independent source review of T6 gave 17 findings (5 high), all fixed with regression tests but one low item. A second round fixed three reproduced cases: distinct requirement-line identities, several item facts in one block, and facts with no evidence staying visible without invented evidence. Both are in `docs/archive/`.

**Budget fix and output-aware chunking (SC-T2b).** `NeuronBudget.settle()` had discarded consumption above the reservation; it now charges the full estimate, records overruns and blocks later reservations. Input bound: 2 bytes per token plus 64 template tokens, calibrated on 14 live requests (4.45 to 5.40 bytes per token, mean 4.73). The chunker sizes requests by expected values (14 per request, an estimate of 42 output tokens per value) and never reads labels. A frozen plan of 21 requests is regenerated by a test. The preflight refused the full-length live run at the default limits, so I made the budget honest before spending anything.

**Live baseline (SC-T4).** RFQ 6 requests (72 claims, 60 correct, 12 differ from label, 0 missed), quotation A 8 requests plus 1 resumed after a 25.0 s timeout (13.6 s on retry), quotation B 7 requests. Strict comparison: 16 of 18 expected findings exact, 2 conservative misses, 4 false critical (a leading "with" kept in quotation B specifications), 0 over-confident. 22 attempts, about 2,906 of 6,000 neurons. The audit also raised a false alarm on "7.5" (punctuation inside words), fixed with tests (`docs/results/sc-t4-scopecheck-baseline.md`).

**Offline correctness fixes (SC-T12 prep).** Leading connectors, identity from validated source cells, coordinate-aware cell detection (parser `ingest/2-cells`), all 96 comparisons planned with Not evaluated rows, separate exclusion findings. Replay of the saved baseline: 18 of 18 expected, 1 false critical (an extraction fault), 96 comparisons (22 Not evaluated). Rules v4 then marked a specification that contains its own row's item code as contaminated: 18 of 18, 0 false critical, 23 Not evaluated (`docs/results/sc-t4-offline-replay-v2.md`). I wrote a fresh pre-registered set (`samples/scopecheck-fresh/`: tender and quotation A positioned tables, quotation B prose, 12 expected findings) and a realistic PackCheck pack (7 expected findings) before any inference.

**Fresh-set validation (SC-T12, 22:55 to 23:12 UTC).** Runner stop conditions and a superseding freeze were committed first. Allowance 16 attempts and 3,000 neurons, concurrency 1, one repair. Two invocations I started in the background died with the tool call, losing 2 attempts and leaving 727.1 neurons of reservations standing; I added a recovery step (`freeze-3`), changed no rule or label, and finished in the foreground. Result: **12 of 12 expected, 0 missed, 0 false critical, 0 over-confident**, 1 unexpected Needs review (an extraction fault), 48 comparisons with 9 Not evaluated. 14 of 16 attempts, 2,109.2 of 3,000 neurons (`docs/results/sc-t12-fresh-results.md`, profile `scopecheck.quote.v1`).

**Second model on the fresh set (SC-T15, 2 Nov).** 8B: 12 of 12 requests valid, findings 3 of 12, recall 28%, 0 false critical. Fails the criteria; the 70B stays. 746.9 neurons (`docs/results/sc-t15-second-model.md`).

Refusals say that nothing more was sent and that the samples still work. 635 tests.

## 3 Nov 2026: samples, theme, live opening

**Coverage hint, banner, recorded budget fixture.** A hint beside fully processed documents explains confirming coverage; banners are one line at narrow widths. The PackCheck budget document was re-extracted once through a token-gated staging Worker (ceiling 1, 200 in 8.3 s, 580 prompt and 254 completion tokens, 74.220 neurons with margin; the Worker's ledger charged 74.237), so the fixture is recorded output again rather than an edited recording.

**Full-length default sample, totals, severity.** `scopecheck-fulllength` is built from the fresh set and the complete SC-T12 recording (123 claims, replayed unchanged, faults included). "Total as quoted" (decimal.js) sums quantity times unit price only when both are validated and unambiguous, flags incomplete totals with the reason per line, never adds different currencies, and ranks nothing. `quantity.differs` is now High; `spec.differs` and `item.absent` stay Critical. Pages redeployed from `53d1d385` (ScopeCheck `cac432f7`, PackCheck `e775418a`). Quotations are named by their fictional supplier.

**Haunted interface (HU-T2 to HU-T6).** Semantic colour utilities everywhere (a test fails on a raw palette class), a distinct icon and text per state, theme names beside plain labels (never in exports), the séance summary, ghost rows, possessed cells, and motifs behind guards. `tools/a11y/check.ts` runs both apps, both modes, at 1280 px and 375 px with axe, focus rings, no sideways scroll, no decoration over text or controls, and no animation under reduced motion. HU-1 to HU-16 are each mapped to the test that covers them. Known limits: checks run on the sample pages and the ghost state, not every live result; screenshots show 1280 px only.

**Clarification lists.** One plain-text list per supplier, from confirmed findings and unresolved items, each marked `[CONFIRMED]` or `[TO CHECK]` with the cited words and line numbers; names only its own supplier and ranks nothing (`clarification.ts`).

**Hooks.** Two save hooks in `.kiro/hooks`: engine module tests, and rerun-golden-fixtures-and-report-changed-outcomes. Neither has fired in a Kiro session; I ran both commands by hand: 56 outcomes unchanged and 42 golden tests passing on unchanged rules, and after a deliberate, reverted change to the quantity severity, 10 outcomes reported changed while the 42 golden tests still passed because they do not pin severity. A manual "update README from specs" has no hook form, so it is `npm run docs:sync` (`docs/hooks.md`).

**Whole-run admission.** Before Analyse sends text, the browser sends the run's plan (schema, request size, expected values; no text) to `POST /run`. The Worker prices the whole run at its worst case (exact input bound plus an enforced output cap of 972 tokens per part, one attempt per part), holds it in one transaction or refuses it up front with the numbers. A held run cannot be refused for budget; its hold falls to actual use when it ends or after 20 minutes; no automatic repair inside a run; at most 3 runs held at once. The cap covers the chunker's 14-value design maximum, because the value estimate for the RFQ's table rows (0 or 1) would have truncated three of the twelve recorded answers. 148 Worker tests. Simulation at 6,000 neurons: full-length ScopeCheck 2 runs a day, small set 13, PackCheck 10 (`docs/results/run-admission-simulation.md`).

**Signature effects.** Candles equal to the Needs review count, a Next finding button with a planchette, an idle whisper, possessed hover glitches, a ghost drift, a wax seal and fade, ink, fog and mist, and an easter egg; all off under Lights on and reduced motion. Measured: composited contrast (a ghost at 20% failed at 4.42:1 and was lowered to 12%, 5.33:1), 37 behaviour tests, every effect driven in a real browser. Next finding: on the live site it focused an item about 11,000 px away without scrolling. I could not reproduce it by clicking, but I reproduced the page staying put when the browser drops a smooth scroll, and fixed it by scrolling first, then focusing, smooth only for short hops.

**Candlelight pass.** Warm lantern light on a near-black page (`#0a0a0d`), original SVG art (`docs/art.md`), amber and orange only in the header, gutters and illustrations (a test enforces it, and a browser check keeps it 24 px from any badge), a key-in-lock unlock (0.8 s, results fade in by 1.25 s), a trick-or-treat pumpkin, Pirata One and Special Elite self-hosted with their licences (38,268 B subset covering Basic Latin, é, curly quotes, dashes and the ellipsis), themed stage names, a themed 404, and a title glow that builds once and holds. Séance numbers are drawn in the data font, because a 1 in Special Elite reads as an l; Accept is neutral, not the green of Agrees.

**Unit and price rules (rules v5).** `unit.ambiguous` (Needs review, High): a missing unit, or one that differs from the tender's with no conversion the documents state ("1 box = 100 each"; "box of 100" is not a conversion). `price.arithmetic` (confirmed, High): a stated line total differs from quantity times unit price (less a stated percentage discount, half-up to cents, one cent tolerated), with `price.unclear` and a Not evaluated row when tax, currencies, units or duplicates make the basis unclear. On the full-length sample, unit ambiguity finds one case (SP-5302, where the recording read "1 l" as the quantity).

**Live analysis opened (6,000 neurons, 80 attempts).** I simulated those values first, then set `OPEN` in `deploy.ts` to them; the per-client limiter (6 requests per 60 s) guards opening a run and single requests but not the parts of an admitted run, so a fast 12-part run is not rate-limited partway; `--verify-open` also checks `/run` from a Pages origin and another origin. Worker `b48bca11`, Pages `06fcdbcc` and `5042fa39` from `84d0f168` (`docs/live-opening.md`).

Note: a CI `a11y` failure on the clarification-list branch was a measurement race (axe ran during the 0.9 s one-time ghost flicker). The check now waits for every finite animation to finish. I had merged before reading that result; I now wait for required checks in a separate step.

## 5 to 7 Nov 2026: first production run, line totals, consumption
